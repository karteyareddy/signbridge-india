# Responsible AI Constraints

**Ethical Guidelines:**
- **No Hallucinations**: Never fabricate AI accuracy or intent predictions. 
- **Mandatory Fallback**: Low-confidence predictions must always trigger a safe fallback UI (e.g., offering manual phrase selection or human assistance).
- **Not a Universal Translator**: Never describe the MVP or the AI as a universal ISL translator. It is a constrained intent-recognition tool.
- **Human Collaboration**: AI does not replace certified interpreters. For complex, high-stakes medical or legal communication, always offer human escalation.
- **Community Validation**: All communication phrases and models should ideally be validated by the Deaf community, not assumed correct.
